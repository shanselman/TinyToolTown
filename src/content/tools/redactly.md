Upload an American Express UK or Barclaycard statement PDF, type keywords for the transactions you want to keep (e.g. `Uber`, `TFL`, `Office Supplies`), and get back a statement with everything else blacked out. Enhanced-privacy mode also strips your name, address, account number, balances, and rewards while keeping the statement structure intact. It's inverse redaction: instead of marking what to hide, you choose what to show.

I built it because landlords, employers, and expense claims ask for "a statement" when they only need to see specific transactions — this way you share exactly that and nothing more. Everything is processed in memory and nothing is stored. No account, no credits, no limits — just free, with the full source on GitHub under MIT.
