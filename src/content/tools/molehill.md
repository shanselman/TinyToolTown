My daily job requires to have multiple VPN's to connect to company/customer networks. It was always a pain to setup/maintain/use 3+ VPN clients, dealing with different settings, interfaces, etc. 

So I built (I mean Claude built, I managed/directed/etc. Otherwise I won't spend time on building it. What a wonderful time to live) Molehill, simple VPN client that works with OpenVPN, Fortinet, Cisco & Paolo Alto Global Connect VPN networks and more to come. I'm using it for about a month now and It's a joy. I already forgot how it felt to use multiple clunky tools. It works with standard auth, with 2FA, with SSO, updates itself and underlying libraries.

I thought there may be others, who also don't enjoy using multiple VPN clients and maybe Molehill will be helpful for them too.
