Plexbar is a keyboard-driven terminal UI for browsing and playing your Plex music library without opening a web browser. It features comprehensive library navigation by artists, albums, tracks, playlists, and genres with search capabilities and album art display. The player supports local playback through mpv with live progress tracking and responsive keyboard-driven controls.
