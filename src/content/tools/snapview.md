SnapView is a lightweight Windows screenshot utility that lives in your notification area, letting you capture a region with a keyboard shortcut and keep it floating on screen for easy reference. You can zoom, drag snaps into other apps, copy, save, or share them with a simple right-click. It's a native, fast, and genuinely useful tool for anyone who takes a lot of screenshots.
