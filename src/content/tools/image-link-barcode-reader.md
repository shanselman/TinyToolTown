Flyers and screenshots often hide URLs and QR codes behind pixels you cannot tap. This small static web app lets you paste, drop, or pick an image; it runs ZXing for barcodes/QR (including multi-scale passes for large posters) and Tesseract.js for OCR with layout so you can copy or open what matters. Everything runs locally in the browser. UI styling is inspired by Tiny Tool Town.
