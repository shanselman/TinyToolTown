Clipboard Next is a tiny macOS and Windows menu bar app for live demos. Queue commands and prompts, then each Command-V loads the next snippet onto your clipboard. Import Markdown presenter notes, track your place with a floating badge, and stay in your flow. Everything stays on your device, and the app never runs your commands.
