The **"Money Burned" command line interface** is a tiny tool that visualizes the money spent over time by knowing the resources used and the time elapsed. Or to put it simply: It's like a stopwatch, except that instead of time, it's money that's slipping away.

Originally designed for educational purposes, the idea behind "Money Burned" was to give people a good understanding of how to develop software - beyond just running a simple Hello World app. One of the results is this console application, which meets the minimum requirements.

What makes it particularly interesting is that "Money Burned" is, in a sense, a cross-purpose project: On one hand, you have a ready-to-use tool - a CLI that runs on Linux and Windows. On the other hand, you can choose a framework of your choice, implement the requirements, and develop your own version.
