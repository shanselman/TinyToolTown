AI Crawler Robots.txt Builder is a free browser-only utility for site owners, SEO operators, and developers who need to prepare robots.txt rules for current AI crawler and AI search user-agent tokens. It can generate policy groups for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, and custom tokens, then audit pasted robots.txt text for common missing or root-level decisions. It is intentionally small, static, and local-first.
