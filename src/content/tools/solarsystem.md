Inspired by the Artemis II mission to the Dark Side Of The Moon, I got Claude to help me write a Solar System and Space Mission Simulation, running in web at https://pcwilliams.design/solarsystem, documented at https://pcwilliams.design/dev/solarsystem-web/architecture.html with full source on GitHub. I didn't know three.js 3d graphics, much web programming or Kepler planetary maths, so this was an awesome learning experience for me. @shanselman's awesome talk at NDC Toronto persuaded me to submit this here!
