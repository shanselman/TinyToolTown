A neon match-three puzzle game with explosive special pieces, chain reactions, a collectable tray, and an AI that plays like a strong human. Swap pieces, line up three or more, and watch the board light up.

The AI was a joy to write. It finds the best match in under 40ms, and you can watch it play for you if you want. It also powers the "hint." I also had fun with the sounds. The thud when you blow up a 4x4 grid sounds amazing on a sub. 

I also added in a macOS native version that you can just put in your Applications folder. At some point, I'll rebuild it in swift and make an iOS version for the App Store.
