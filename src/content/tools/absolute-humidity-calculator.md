Absolute Humidity Calculator is a simple web tool that helps you decide whether opening a window will make your indoor air drier or more humid. Enter the temperature and relative humidity measured inside and outside, and the tool converts both readings into absolute humidity for a meaningful comparison. It then gives a clear ventilation recommendation and visualizes the difference, turning confusing weather data into a practical, everyday decision.
