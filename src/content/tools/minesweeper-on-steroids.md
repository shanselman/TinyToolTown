Minesweeper on Steroids takes the familiar Windows classic and gives it an arcade-sized energy boost. Play Classic for pure deduction, unleash shields and radar in Steroids mode, tackle a repeatable Daily challenge, or conquer five increasingly dangerous sectors in Arcade Run—earning energy, deploying abilities, and choosing upgrades along the way.

I built it to answer one deeply unnecessary but important question: what if Minesweeper had more firepower? The result is a native Windows desktop game with glossy neon tiles, animated reveal waves, explosion effects, victory confetti, and original synthesized sounds. Prefer quieter chaos? Sound and reduced-motion toggles are included.

It runs entirely offline, needs no account, and saves scores locally. The standalone Windows x64 build includes the runtime. Your first click is safe. Your ego is not.
