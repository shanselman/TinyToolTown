SaaS Pricing Page Confidence Checklist is a free browser-only utility for founders, marketers, and product teams reviewing whether a SaaS pricing page is clear enough to earn qualified trial or demo clicks. It scores pricing clarity, plan packaging, buyer proof, objection handling, risk reversal, and conversion friction, then produces prioritized gaps plus Markdown and CSV exports. It is intentionally small, static, and local-first.
