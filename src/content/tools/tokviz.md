tokviz lets you instantly visualize LLM token usage and costs in real time, with zero configuration. It receives OpenTelemetry traces, filters for LLM spans, and displays a live dashboard—no database, Docker, or persistence. Perfect for developers who want clear, focused insight into LLM spend without heavy tooling.
