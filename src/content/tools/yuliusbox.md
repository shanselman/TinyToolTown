YuliusBox is a collection of 28 free browser-based tools — image conversion and compression, PDF utilities, ID watermarking, EXIF removal, JSON/SQL formatters and more. Everything runs client-side in the browser: files are never uploaded and there is no account to create. Open source under the MIT license, and self-hostable as a Next.js app.
