Learn Mode is a Claude Code skill that watches what the AI does during your coding session, then explains the concepts, saves structured notes, and helps you build a personal knowledge base over time — all with a single `/learn` command.

After the AI completes a task, say `/learn` and it becomes your mentor — breaking down what happened, naming the concepts, and saving notes you can revisit later. Notes are saved, organized, and reviewable in one searchable knowledge base at `~/learning-notes/`. Also supports organizing and reviewing notes so you can track your growth over time.
