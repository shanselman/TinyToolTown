Copilot Journey TUI transforms your local Copilot CLI session history into an interactive, offline terminal dashboard. Explore your progress through phases, habits, and personalized tips to grow your skills, all visualized across five insightful tabs. Built with Textual and themed with Catppuccin Mocha, it offers actionable recommendations and deep dives into your usage patterns. Enjoy a privacy-first experience—nothing leaves your machine.
