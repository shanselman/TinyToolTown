When I'm working at my developer workstation, my laptop sits right beside me running Teams and Outlook. I don't want to keep tweaking sleep/lock settings on it every time, so this tool just keeps it active while I'm at the desk — no policy changes, no fuss. As a nice side effect, Teams also stays "Available" instead of flipping to Away, since Windows never sees the machine as idle. The tool lives in the Windows taskbar tray — once started it stays out of the way.
