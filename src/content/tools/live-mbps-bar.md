Live Mbps Bar is a simple iOS app that displays real-time network speed as a small bar at the top of Safari.
The bar grows and shrinks with network activity, making it easy to see when data is being transferred.
I built it because I wanted a simple way to visualize network activity without opening a separate speed test app.
It's free, open source, and doesn't require a VPN or an account.
