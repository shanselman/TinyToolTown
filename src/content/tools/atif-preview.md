ATIF Preview lets you see exactly what your AI agent did by turning raw ATIF trajectory JSON into an interactive, step-by-step timeline inside VS Code. Instantly inspect messages, tool calls, reasoning traces, and token costs with a native, theme-aware interface. Diagnose agent behavior in seconds, not minutes, and navigate even complex multi-agent runs with ease.
