Pokey for Windows is a free, open-source .NET 10 WPF tray app that makes the mouse pointer easier—and more fun—to follow during demos, recordings, and screen shares. Press Ctrl+Alt+P to toggle it; as clicks arrive faster, the click-through overlay escalates through hand-drawn poke animations and impact bursts across mixed-DPI, multi-monitor setups. Signed, self-contained x64 and ARM64 releases are available, so no separate .NET installation is required.
