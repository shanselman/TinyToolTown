Micro-SaaS Runway Price Floor Calculator is a free browser-only utility for solo founders and tiny SaaS operators checking whether current monthly ARPA can cover overhead, founder income, growth budget, churn, and gross margin. It estimates a survival price floor, break-even customer count, monthly margin gap, and cash-runway pressure, then produces local Markdown and CSV exports. It is intentionally small, static, and local-first.
