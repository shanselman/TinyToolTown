I wanted the useful parts of YouTube, Instagram, X and LinkedIn without the endless feeds, so I built one small extension for Chrome and Brave that I can tune myself. It hides Home feeds, Shorts and recommended videos, but keeps search, messages and subscriptions. When you really want the feed, you can show it for 1, 3 or 5 minutes. Press ← on YouTube or Netflix to rewind five seconds. It can also hide any element you pick on a page, show stats on Instagram posts, and block those annoying popups on streaming sites. It works without an account, and your settings stay in your browser.
