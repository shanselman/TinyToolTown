Bananify is a free, open-source Chrome and Edge extension that turns everyday websites into a banana party: animated banana rain, click confetti, three random monkey guests, and reversible banana disguises for images and short text.

Made by James Montemagno & Mooch for the joy of putting bananas everywhere. Includes More bananas, Pause, Restore page, and reduced-motion support. Forms, links, navigation, and editable content are excluded from disguises.

Try the live demo without installing. The extension is a manual-install GitHub Release ZIP, not yet a store listing. No runtime dependencies, analytics, or extension network requests. Page content is processed locally.
