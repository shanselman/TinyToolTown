justtoday is a chat-based study tracker. You paste a plan — a markdown outline, a ChatGPT export, or just a few sentences about what you want to learn — and it turns it into a day-by-day sequence. Each day is a single chat: it tells you today's goal, tutors you through the topic, asks how it went, and quietly adapts the rest of the plan to your pace.

I built it because every study tracker I tried wanted me to manage a dashboard, a streak, or a Kanban board. I just wanted to know what to do today. The whole UI is one chat column — no charts, no progress bars, no gamification.

The delightful part: when you say "today was rough, I didn't really get the subjunctive," it doesn't shrug — it inserts a catch-up day and pushes the rest back. The plan bends to your week, not the other way around.
