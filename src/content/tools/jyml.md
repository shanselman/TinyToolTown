jyml is a tiny CLI tool that instantly converts YAML to JSON and vice versa with a single command—no flags or setup required. It swaps file extensions automatically, keeps your directory tidy, and supports structured output for AI agents. With just two dependencies and a footprint under 4 kB, jyml is fast, predictable, and perfect for automation or everyday use.
