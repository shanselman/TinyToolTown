tvdinner plays M3U/M3U8 IPTV playlists — or logs straight into an Xtream Codes panel, a Stalker Portal, or an HDHomeRun tuner, or browses a Plex Media Server's library — through mpv, with a TiviMate-style on-screen EPG overlay and a full program guide built from your XMLTV data — channels, timeline, poster art, favorites, DVR-style recording, and more, all from the terminal.
