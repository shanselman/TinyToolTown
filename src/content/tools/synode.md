Synode is a desktop app for macOS and Windows where several AI models from different providers discuss your question as a council. Each model gives its take, they respond to each other, and a master model you choose reads the whole debate and delivers one final verdict. You can follow up with any council member through @mentions, or switch to Direct Chat for a one-on-one conversation.

It also runs local models, so you can mix cloud and on-device models in the same council or keep everything on your machine. A Telegram integration lets you put questions to the council from your phone. The settings let you pick your models and master model, manage API keys, and tune the prompts each model works from. A setup wizard walks you through it on first launch.

It is surprisingly fun to watch models disagree, and the verdict is often better than any single answer.
